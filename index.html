How to use this graph

  • Scroll to pan the graph, or drag the background. Hold Ctrl/⌘ while scrolling (or pinch) to zoom.
  • Hover a node to highlight its direct callers and callees.
  • Click a node to pin everything connected to it; click it again or click the empty background to clear.
  • Right-click a function to open it in a side tab, or a function, file, or folder to hide it. Tick Show Hidden to bring hidden items back.
  • Search functions, files, and folders in the top bar (Ctrl+F); press Enter to jump through matches.
  • Data search: switch to Data (or type @) and search a field or parameter, e.g. @rho or @t_nh_prog%vn. Choosing a result shows its lifecycle; while the trace is active the graph shows the data path and dims unrelated call edges. The earliest event is marked Created here when a create exists, otherwise First known use.
  • Parameters: click a function to reveal its <N> params pill, use P, or right-click and choose Show parameters. Click a parameter to trace it back to its origin; use the expand icon for the full create/write/read/pass flow.
  • Open and close boxes to see what folders and files contain which functions.
